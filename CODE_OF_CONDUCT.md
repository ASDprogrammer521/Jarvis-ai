# 🛡️ Jarvis-AI Discord Community Code of Conduct

## 🌟 Our Pledge
We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community and Discord server ([Join Here](https://discord.com/invite/r5q93NpRPt)) a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

---

## 📌 Our Standards
Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment in our server include:
- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people.
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences in voice and text channels.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes, and learning from the experience.
- Focusing on what is best not just for us as individuals, but for the overall community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:
- The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment of members (including via Discord Direct Messages).
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission.
- Spamming, flooding channels, or sharing malicious links/files.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional or friendly setting.

---

## ⚖️ Enforcement Responsibilities
Discord server moderators and administrators are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Moderators have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject messages, media, voice channel access, and other contributions that are not aligned to this Code of Conduct, and will communicate reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate.

---

## 🌐 Scope
This Code of Conduct applies within all Discord channels (text and voice), event stages, and also applies when an individual is officially representing the community in other public spaces or social media accounts.

---

## 📯 Enforcement
Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior on the Discord server can be reported directly to the server administrators or moderators. 
* **Discord Invite:** [Join our server](https://discord.com/invite/r5q93NpRPt)
* All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, ensuring the reporter's privacy and security.

---

## 🪜 Enforcement Guidelines
Community leaders will follow these Community Impact Guidelines in determining the consequences for any action in violation of this Code of Conduct:

1. **Correction:** Private warning from moderators for minor inappropriate language or unwelcoming behavior.
2. **Warning:** Official warning with temporary restrictions from specific channels for repeated actions.
3. **Temporary Ban:** Temporary ban from the Discord server for serious violations or sustained inappropriate behavior.
4. **Permanent Ban:** Permanent ban from the community for severe harassment, aggression, or a repeated pattern of rule-breaking.
